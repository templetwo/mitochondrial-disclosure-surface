# Cover Letter — EMBO Reports

[Date]

To the Editors, *EMBO Reports*

Dear Editors,

I am writing to submit a manuscript entitled **"The Mitochondrial Disclosure Surface: VDAC1 Oligomerization as a Unified Sensitization Axis"** for consideration as an **Exploratory Report**.

**The central proposal.** Mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization (MOMP) is conventionally modeled as a set of parallel processes: VDAC1 oligomeric pores for sublethal inflammatory mtDNA disclosure, and mechanistically separate BAX/BAK macropores for terminal apoptotic commitment. This manuscript argues that these regimes are not parallel but sequential — graded outcomes along a single sensitization axis set by VDAC1 oligomerization. The mechanistic keystone is recent structural evidence (Daniilidis et al, 2025, *Nat Commun*) that the exposed VDAC1 N-terminal helix occupies the BH3-binding groove of Bcl-xL, acting as a sensitizer-type ligand. We integrate this into a model in which sustained N-terminal exposure neutralizes Bcl-xL, derepresses BAX/BAK, and thereby couples the inflammatory and apoptotic arms of mitochondrial disclosure through one upstream event.

**Why this fits *EMBO Reports*.** The proposal is a provocative, broadly relevant hypothesis about a node — VDAC1 — central to cancer, neurodegeneration, sterile inflammation, and innate immunity. Crucially, it is framed to be *falsifiable*: we specify a single multiplexed stress-titration experiment and state, in advance, three outcomes that would refute the sequential model and three that would support it. The manuscript also derives concrete pharmacological corollaries, including a re-audit imperative for the widely used tool compound VBIT-4 and a mechanistic account of the two-register behavior of cannabidiol.

**A note on scope and the data question.** I want to be transparent: this is a synthesis-and-hypothesis manuscript that reinterprets existing primary literature; it does not report new primary datasets. I recognize that Exploratory Reports are intended to carry initial reproducible data, and I would welcome the editors' guidance on whether the contribution is better suited to consideration as an Exploratory Report on the strength of its falsifiable experimental design, or whether a presubmission discussion or alternative format would be more appropriate. I would rather raise this directly than have it surface at triage.

**Novelty and competing work.** To my knowledge, no prior synthesis places VDAC1 oligomerization upstream of BAX/BAK as a single sensitization axis unifying the inflammatory and apoptotic disclosure regimes, nor frames the outer membrane as a continuously publishing "disclosure surface" with an explicit five-state spectrum. The framework is original to this work. There are no associated or competing manuscripts under consideration elsewhere.

The manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration at another journal. I am the sole author and declare no competing interests. The full theoretical framework from which this report is condensed is openly archived (Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20373134).

Thank you for considering this submission. I am happy to provide any further information.
